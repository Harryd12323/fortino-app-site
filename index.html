    Your plan preview

    Here's what getting started could look like

    1. Free consult. Talk through your goals, history and questions with Dr. Fortino's team.
    2. Health review. Your physician reviews your health history and may order labs to make sure a program is safe for you.
    3. Your plan. If it's appropriate, your physician builds a plan, which may or may not include a GLP-1 medication, alongside food and activity guidance.
    4. Regular check-ins. Track progress between visits and adjust the plan together.

    This preview is general information based on your answers, not a diagnosis or a promise of results. Individual results vary.

        Privacy & your data

        This is a preview version of the app.

        • Quiz answers, name and email stay in this browser tab and are gone when you close it. They aren't sent anywhere.
        • Habits, check-ins, waist entries and wins are saved on this device. If you sign in (under More), they are also saved to a test account so they sync across your devices. Signing out removes them from this device. Test accounts are for made-up information only.
        • Membership and consult forms don't send anything yet.

        Before the practice uses this app with patients, these will be connected to secure, HIPAA-compliant systems and this page will be replaced with the practice's privacy notice.
