          How hard should it feel? The talk test

          Easy
          You can chat comfortably.
          Moderate
          You're breathing faster but can still speak in sentences. You can talk, but not sing.
          Too hard for now
          You can only say a few words, your form is slipping, or you feel unwell. Slow down or stop.

          Aim for easy to moderate. You don't need to push to exhaustion or until you can't do another.

            This is a preview version of the app.

            • Quiz answers, name and email stay in this browser tab and are gone when you close it. They aren't sent anywhere.
            • Habits, check-ins, waist entries, wins and your Move starting-point answers are saved on this device. If you sign in (under More), they are also saved to a test account so they sync across your devices. Signing out removes them from this device. Test accounts are for made-up information only.

            Before the practice uses this app with patients, these will be connected to secure, HIPAA-compliant systems and this page will be replaced with the practice's privacy notice.

            HIPAA

            This test version hasn't been set up for HIPAA. No business associate agreement (BAA) is in place with the services it uses. That's why anything you enter here should be test information only.

            What the app collects

            • Your email address, if you sign in. It's used to send you sign-in emails and to identify your account.
            • What you enter: habit ticks, check-ins (energy, hunger, stomach), waist measurements, wins, your goal, your planned workout time, and your Move "starting point" answers (age range, goal, biggest struggle, equipment, time per session, and whether you were asked to check with your care team). Which symptoms you tick in that quiz isn't saved.
            • Your journal: the entries you write or dictate, the labels you add to them, and when you wrote them. The app doesn't record audio: speaking an entry uses your phone keyboard's own dictation, which follows your phone maker's privacy settings.
            • Readiness quiz answers, name and email stay in the open browser tab only. They aren't saved or sent anywhere.
            • Membership and consult request forms don't send or save anything yet.

            Where it's stored

            • On your device, in your browser's storage.
            • If you sign in, your habits, check-ins, waist entries, wins and Move starting-point answers are also stored in a database run by Supabase, an outside service, so they can sync across your devices. Your goal, planned workout time and journal stay on your device only.
            • The app is hosted on GitHub Pages. [To be added: what the hosting and database services may record, such as standard server logs, based on their own policies.]

            This test version doesn't include advertising or analytics tools.

            Your choices

            • You can use the app without signing in. Your information then stays on your device.
            • Privacy & your data has a button that deletes everything the app saved on this device and, if you're signed in, the data synced to your account.
            • Signing out removes synced data from this device.
            • Removing your sign-in account itself (your email address) isn't available in the app yet. To ask for it, contact [to be added].
